# MyFundedFutures: automation quick check (2026-10-28)

Grade: OFFICIAL for the policy text. No rule numbers were collected, as requested.

Source: https://help.myfundedfutures.com/en/articles/8444599-fair-play-and-prohibited-trading-practices (date on page 2026-08-24)
- "High-frequency Trading is not allowed on our plans."
- "Traders may make use of automated trading strategies tailored to their own specific settings", as long as they don't target the simulator's favourable fills (exploiting no-slippage or tight brackets is banned).
- "Traders trading live accounts with automated trading must abide by CME guidelines."
- "Tier 1 Economic Data Trading: Engaging in trades during tier 1 economic data releases is restricted."
- Copy trading between traders is banned, and two traders can't use the same device.

Blogs (TradersPost, FundedTrading) say only *semi*-automated trading is allowed and fully autonomous bots are banned (200+ trades/day = HFT). **The official article doesn't say "semi-automated only"**, so the conflict is unresolved.

Platforms (help centre titles): Tradovate, NinjaTrader, TradingView, Volumetrica, DEEPCHART, ATAS, Quantower. No API is documented.

Verdict: automation is officially allowed in general terms, but blogs report a ban on fully unattended bots, and there's no Python API. Low priority; not drafted as JSON.
