# Efficient Estimation of Bid–Ask Spreads from OHLC Prices (Ardia, Guidotti, Kroencke)

**Citation:** Ardia, D., Guidotti, E., Kroencke, T.A. (2024). Efficient estimation of bid–ask spreads from open, high, low, and close prices. Journal of Financial Economics 161, 103916.  
**Free copy:** https://irf.fhnw.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/e8c469d2-9c64-4004-8e87-53ee091f0cb1/content  
**Version:** Published JFE version (open access, CC BY) via FHNW repository

## Key points (verbatim quotes)

1. **Older estimators biased**  
   > "Popular bid–ask spread estimators are downward biased when trading is infrequent." (PDF p.1)

2. **CS vs AR variance**  
   > "the estimator by Corwin and Schultz (2012) has a lower variance than the estimator by Abdi and Ranaldo (2017) for small spreads, while it has a higher variance for large spreads." (PDF p.2)
