# Value and Momentum Everywhere (Asness, Moskowitz, Pedersen)

**Citation:** Asness, C.S., Moskowitz, T.J., Pedersen, L.H. (2013). Value and Momentum Everywhere. Journal of Finance 68(3), 929–985.  
**Free copy:** https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~lpederse/papers/ValMomEverywhere.pdf  
**Version:** Author-hosted, published JF version

## Key points (each backed by verbatim quote)

1. **Premia across eight markets**  
   > "We find consistent value and momentum return premia across eight diverse markets and asset classes, and a strong common factor structure among their returns." (PDF p.1)

2. **Value and momentum negatively correlated**  
   > "value and momentum are negatively correlated with each other, both within and across asset classes." (PDF p.1)

3. **Funding liquidity link**  
   > "Global funding liquidity risk is a partial source of these patterns" (PDF p.1)
