# Inefficiencies in the Pricing of Exchange-Traded Funds (Petajisto)

**Citation:** Petajisto, A. (2017). Inefficiencies in the Pricing of Exchange-Traded Funds. Financial Analysts Journal 73(1), 24–54.  
**Free copy:** https://www.petajisto.net/papers/etf28.pdf  
**Version:** Author draft, Aug 29, 2011

## Key points (verbatim quotes)

1. **ETF prices deviate from NAV**  
   > "The prices of exchange-traded funds can deviate significantly from their net asset values, in spite of the arbitrage mechanism" (PDF p.1)

2. **Worse for illiquid/international holdings**  
   > "The deviations are larger in funds holding international or illiquid securities" (PDF p.1)

## Relevance to Quant Agent Brain
SPY/QQQ/IWM hold liquid US stocks, so premium/discount noise should be small; low relevance except as a check that IWM (small caps) is the most likely to show NAV noise.
