# Optimal Execution of Portfolio Transactions (Almgren & Chriss)

**Citation:** Almgren, R., Chriss, N. (2000/2001). Optimal Execution of Portfolio Transactions. Journal of Risk 3(2), 5–39.  
**Free copy:** https://www.smallake.kr/wp-content/uploads/2016/03/optliq.pdf  
**Version:** December 2000 preprint (42 pp.). Hosted on a third-party site already in the reading list; author copy may also exist at math.nyu.edu/~almgren — reviewer should swap to author host if preferred.

## Key points (each backed by verbatim quote)

1. **Objective: impact cost vs volatility risk**  
   > "We consider the execution of portfolio transactions with the aim of minimizing a combination of volatility risk and transaction costs aris- ing from permanent and temporary market impact." (PDF p.1)

2. **Efficient frontier of liquidation schedules**  
   > "we explicitly construct the efficient frontier in the space of time-dependent liquidation strategies, which have minimum expected cost for a given level of uncertainty." (PDF p.1)

3. **Liquidity-adjusted VaR**  
   > "The latter choice leads to the concept of Liquidity-adjusted VAR, or L-VaR, that explicitly considers the best tradeoff between volatility risk and liquidation costs." (PDF p.1)
