# Time Series Momentum (Moskowitz, Ooi, Pedersen)

**Citation:** Moskowitz, T.J., Ooi, Y.H., Pedersen, L.H. (2012). Time Series Momentum. Journal of Financial Economics 104, 228–250.  
**Free copy:** https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~lpederse/papers/TimeSeriesMomentum.pdf

## Key points (each backed by verbatim quote)

1. **Persistence 1–12 months, later partial reversal**  
   > "persistence in returns for one to 12 months that partially reverses over longer horizons" (PDF p.1)

2. **Diversified TSMOM: abnormal returns, low factor exposure**  
   > "delivers substantial abnormal returns with little exposure to standard asset pricing" (PDF p.1)

3. **Own past excess return predicts future return**  
   > "excess return of each instrument is a positive predictor of" (PDF p.1)
