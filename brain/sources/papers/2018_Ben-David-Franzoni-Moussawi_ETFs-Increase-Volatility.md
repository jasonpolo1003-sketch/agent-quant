# Do ETFs Increase Volatility? (Ben-David, Franzoni, Moussawi)

**Citation:** Ben-David, I., Franzoni, F., Moussawi, R. (2018). Do ETFs Increase Volatility? Journal of Finance 73(6), 2471–2535.  
**Free copy:** https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w20071/w20071.pdf  
**Version:** NBER Working Paper 20071, rev. June 2014

## Key points (verbatim quotes)

1. **Higher volatility from ETF ownership**  
   > "stocks owned by ETFs exhibit significantly higher intraday and daily volatility." (PDF p.2)

2. **Arbitrage channel**  
   > "The driving channel appears to be arbitrage activity between ETFs and the underlying stocks." (PDF p.2)
