# Volatility Managed Portfolios (Moreira & Muir)

**Citation:** Moreira, A., Muir, T. (2017). Volatility-Managed Portfolios. Journal of Finance 72(4), 1611–1644.  
**Free copy:** https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w22208/w22208.pdf  
**Version:** NBER Working Paper 22208, April 2016 rev. June 2016 (not peer-reviewed version)

## Key points (each backed by verbatim quote)

1. **Lower risk when vol is high raises Sharpe**  
   > "Managed portfolios that take less risk when volatility is high produce large alphas, substantially increase factor Sharpe ratios, and produce large utility gains for mean-variance investors." (PDF p.2)

2. **Why it works**  
   > "Volatility timing increases Sharpe ratios because changes in factor volatilities are not offset by proportional changes in expected returns." (PDF p.2)

3. **Takes less risk in crises**  
   > "it takes relatively less risk in recessions and crises yet still earns high average returns." (PDF p.2)
