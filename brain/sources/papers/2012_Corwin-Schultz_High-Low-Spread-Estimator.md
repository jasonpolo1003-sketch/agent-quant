# A Simple Way to Estimate Bid-Ask Spreads from Daily High and Low Prices (Corwin & Schultz)

**Citation:** Corwin, S.A., Schultz, P. (2012). A simple way to estimate bid-ask spreads from daily high and low prices. Journal of Finance 67(2), 719–760.  
**Free copy:** https://users.nber.org/~confer/2009/mms09/Corwin_Schultz.pdf  
**Version:** 2009 NBER Market Microstructure conference draft

## Key points (verbatim quotes)

1. **Intuition**  
   > "Daily high (low) prices are almost always buy (sell) orders." (PDF p.2)

2. **Two-day trick**  
   > "When high-low price ratios are estimated over two days, the variance is twice as large but the bid-ask spread component is unchanged." (PDF p.2)
