# Have Capital Market Anomalies Attenuated in the Recent Era of High Liquidity? (Chordia, Subrahmanyam, Tong)

**Citation:** Chordia, T., Subrahmanyam, A., Tong, Q. (2014). Have capital market anomalies attenuated in the recent era of high liquidity and trading activity? Journal of Accounting and Economics 58(1), 41–58.  
**Free copy:** https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/soc/wbs/subjects/finance/events/seminars/cst.pdf  
**Version:** Author working version dated Feb 12, 2014 (seminar copy hosted by Warwick)

## Key points (verbatim quotes)

1. **Anomalies roughly halved after decimalization**  
   > "the average returns from a portfolio strategy based on prominent anomalies have approximately halved after decimalization." (PDF p.2)

2. **Arbitrage proxies drive the decline**  
   > "hedge fund assets under management, short interest and aggregate share turnover have led to the decline in anomaly-based trading strategy profits in recent years." (PDF p.2)

## Relevance to Quant Agent Brain
Mechanism for decay: more arbitrage capital and cheaper trading.
