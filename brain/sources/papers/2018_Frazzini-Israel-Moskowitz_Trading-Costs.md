# Trading Costs (Frazzini, Israel, Moskowitz)

**Citation:** Frazzini, A., Israel, R., Moskowitz, T.J. (2018). Trading Costs. Working paper, AQR / Yale (SSRN 3229719).  
**Free copy:** https://spinup-000d1a-wp-offload-media.s3.amazonaws.com/faculty/wp-content/uploads/sites/3/2021/08/Trading-Cost.pdf  
**Version:** Draft Aug 2018, hosted on Yale faculty site media bucket (Moskowitz)

## Key points (verbatim quotes)

1. **Real costs much lower than literature**  
   > "We find actual trading costs to be an order of magnitude smaller than previous studies suggest" (PDF p.1)

2. **Live data**  
   > "Using 1.7 trillion dollars of live trade execution data from a large institutional money manager across 21 developed equity markets over a 19-year period" (PDF p.1)

## Relevance to Quant Agent Brain
Cost calibration: academic spread estimates can overstate real execution costs for patient institutional trading. Not ETF-specific.
