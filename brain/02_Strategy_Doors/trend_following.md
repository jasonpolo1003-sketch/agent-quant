# Trend Following Door

## What this method is

Trend following strategies try to participate when prices are moving persistently in one direction.

## Common variations

- Moving average crossover
- Price above/below moving average
- Breakout systems
- Time-series momentum
- Volatility targeting

## Required data

- Historical OHLCV prices
- Benchmark returns
- Transaction cost assumptions

## Known failure modes

- Whipsaw losses in sideways markets.
- Slow exits during crashes.
- Overfitted moving-average windows.
- Looks good because of buy-and-hold exposure, not real edge.
