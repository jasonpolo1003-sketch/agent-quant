## Summary

*   The U.S. economy remains in ERM's Stable regime, with no confirmed structural deterioration.
*   Historically, SPY rose in about 88% of Stable quarters, and QQQ in about 85%.
*   Transitions out of Stable have generally taken one to two quarters to develop, leaving room for selective, temporary leverage.
*   ERM Dynamic Allocation produced a 20.42% CAGR with a 21.50% maximum drawdown in the 2015–2026 SPY/SPXL test.
*   I favor adding leverage after pullbacks in Stable, then reducing exposure as the macro regime deteriorates.
