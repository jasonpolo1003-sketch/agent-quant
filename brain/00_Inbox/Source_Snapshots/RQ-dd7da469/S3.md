## The RSI-25 Strategy for ETFs (Larry Connors)

The RSI-25 adapts mean-reversion for **Exchange-Traded Funds** in long-term uptrends experiencing short-term corrections. Developed by **Larry Connors**, it exploits the fact that ETFs, as diversified baskets of stocks, revert to the mean more reliably than individual stocks.

## Why ETFs Are Ideal for Mean Reversion

Diversification effect: no single company event can permanently damage an ETF

Institutional rebalancing: large funds rebalance into oversold ETFs, creating buying pressure

Stronger mean reversion: individual stocks can go to zero; diversified ETFs almost never do

## The Complete Rules

### Indicators Required

RSI-25 for ETFs: 4-period RSI with 200-SMA trend filter, optional scale-in below 20, exit above 55

### Entry and Exit

1/5

#### Trend Filter

ETF price must be ABOVE the 200-day SMA.

Scaling Risk

Adding to a losing position is dangerous in most contexts. Here it works because the trend filter (200-day SMA) confirms the long-term trend is intact. Never add to a losing position in an ETF that is below its 200-day SMA.

### Stop-Loss

Similar to RSI-2, Connors' backtesting shows fixed stops reduce profitability. Apply your L4 position sizing rules: risk 0.5-1% per position, and use a disaster stop at 8-10% below entry for catastrophic protection.

## Performance Characteristics

*   **Average holding period:** 3-7 days
*   **Win rate:** 70-80%
*   **Average win:** +2% to +4%
*   **Average loss:** -1.5% to -3%
*   **Profit factor:** 2.0 - 3.0

## Best ETF Candidates

**Broad Market (Best)** — SPY (S&P 500), QQQ (Nasdaq 100), DIA (Dow Jones), IWM (Russell 2000)

**Sector (Good)** — XLK (Technology), XLF (Financials), XLE (Energy), XLV (Healthcare), XLI (Industrials)

**International (Acceptable)** — EFA (Developed International), EEM (Emerging Markets)

Avoid leveraged ETFs (2x, 3x). Decay and volatility make mean reversion unreliable.

Avoid inverse ETFs. They confuse the trend filter.

Avoid commodity ETFs. Contango/backwardation distorts price action.

Avoid thinly-traded niche ETFs. Low volume means poor execution.

Key Takeaways

*   RSI-25 uses 4-period RSI with a threshold of 25, designed specifically for ETFs in long-term uptrends
*   Exit when 4-period RSI rises above 55; optional scale-in if RSI drops below 20
*   Win rate of 70-80% makes this one of the most comfortable strategies psychologically
*   ETFs revert more reliably than individual stocks due to diversification
*   Focus on high-liquidity broad market and sector ETFs; avoid leveraged and commodity ETFs
