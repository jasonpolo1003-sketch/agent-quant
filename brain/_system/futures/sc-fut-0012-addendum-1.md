# SC-FUT-0012 addendum 1 (2026-10-29, before any result)

Written by the engineering agent after reading spec v1 (sha256 5cf6d4ac..., commit 7a51f9e) and before any code ran on market data. Spec v1 and its hash stay as they are. These are mechanical readings of the frozen text, not new parameters. Only the data check from step 1 had been run: the hash-checked dev file holds overnight Globex bars (median 930 of the 930 minutes 17:00..08:29 CT per trade date). No P&L of any kind had been computed.

1. **Holiday skip (the one reading with real effect).** Spec: "a calendar gap (holiday) between the evening and D -> skip ($0)". The overnight for D always starts on the previous calendar evening (Sunday's for Monday), so read word for word, no holiday can ever sit between that evening and D, and the clause would do nothing. Reading used: D is skipped ($0, evaluable, both trials) when a weekday (Mon-Fri) that is not an NYSE session lies strictly between the previous NYSE session P and D. Put another way, the 17:00 reopen that follows P starts a non-NYSE trade date, so a holiday sits between that evening and D. Examples: Tuesday after Memorial Day, Friday after Thanksgiving, Monday after Good Friday, 26 Dec, 2 Jan. Weekends alone never skip ("Sunday's for Monday"). This also keeps T2 from acting on a previous session that is several days old.
2. **Order of the day rules.** No overnight bars at all on D's contract means the day is ineligible and dropped, as in every other family; this only happens before the data starts on 2019-05-05. Then the holiday skip (no_trade, $0). Then the missing rule: a missing 17:00 or 08:29 bar makes the day non-evaluable in both trials, so T1 and T2 share one set of evaluable days. Then T2's condition.
3. **T2's previous RTH return** = close of the last bar starting before P's regular close (15:00 CT; 12:15 CT on early-close days, from the frozen calendar) minus the open of P's 08:30 bar. This is the same "regular close" that build_days uses for prev_close. It is read on D's contract, the volume-roll contract that build_days picks for D, which is also how build_days takes prev_close. If P's 08:30 bar or its regular close is missing, the "< 0" condition is not established, so there is no trade ($0). A return of exactly 0 is no trade.
4. **Entry and exit.** Long 1 at the open of the 17:00 CT bar that starts D's trade date (CME trade date = CT + 7h). Out at the open of D's 08:29 CT bar. There is no stop. Missing minutes between the two bars change nothing.
5. **Control.** Direction per draw is `rng.integers(0, 2, size=500) * 2 - 1` (1 is long, 0 is short), the same as SC-FUT-0002. `rng = Generator(PCG64(SeedSequence([20261029, ROOT_CODE[root], yyyymmdd(D), 12])))`; numpy's `PCG64([..])` is the same stream. Each draw uses the same entry and exit bars and the same costs. On a no-trade or skipped day the control is 0. The daily control is the mean of the draws (workbench inference).
6. **RTH diagnostic** ("always-long RTH 08:30 open -> 15:00 close on the same sessions"). Long at D's 08:30 open, out at the close of D's last bar starting before its regular close. That is the 14:59 bar on normal days; on early-close days it is the 12:14 bar, because there is no 15:00 close. L3 cost, same contract. "Same sessions" means each trial's evaluable traded sessions; per evaluable day is also shown. Sessions missing either bar are left out and counted. This is a diagnostic only, not a gate.
7. **Gates** are the spec's: p < 0.0253 (one-sided paired stationary bootstrap, workbench `inference.paired_test`), mean daily L3 net >= $5.00 over all evaluable sessions, with $0 days included, and annual Sharpe of daily L3 net (same days) >= 1.0. The workbench's honesty counter multiplies FAMILY_SPLIT (2) by the distinct trials logged, so the CLI prints a stricter bar after the first run. The spec bar of 0.0253 each governs, and both bars are reported. The workbench's missing-minute alarm (>1% non-evaluable days means "inconclusive") is not a spec gate and is reported separately.
8. **Equity path** (prop-simulator records only, not a gate). It walks the overnight minutes in clock order across midnight (17:00..23:59, then 00:00..08:29). Each bar inside the trade counts its adverse and favourable extremes, and the exit bar counts its open.
9. **Run order.** MNQ T1, then MNQ T2, then MES T1 and T2 (diagnostics, not gated).
