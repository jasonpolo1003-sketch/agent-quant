# SC-FUT-0013 addendum 1 (2026-10-29, before any result)

Written by the engineering agent after reading spec v1 (sha256 cbba76d5..., commit 06dab19) and before any code ran on market data for a P&L. Spec v1 and its hash stay as they are. These are mechanical readings of the frozen text, not new parameters. Only data-availability checks had been run: the ZN volume-roll dates (multi.volume_roll) and which ZN minutes exist on early-close days (ZN trades through the 12:14 CT bar on 2019-11-29, 2019-12-24, 2020-11-27 and 2024-11-29). No return, signal value or P&L of any kind had been computed.

1. **Month positions.** Counted among the NYSE sessions of the frozen calendar (session-calendar.v1.csv) within each calendar month: L is the month's last session, L-1..L-3 the sessions before it, F1..F3 the month's first three sessions. Holidays and weekends are simply not sessions, so a month whose last calendar day is a holiday ends on the session before (May 2021: L = 28 May; March 2024: L = 28 March, Good Friday 29 March). An early-close session is a normal session for positions (Nov 2019 and Nov 2024: L = the 12:15 CT early close on the 29th). The calendar starts on 2019-05-01, the first session of May 2019, so May 2019 is a complete month. A month is never labelled when the calendar does not cover its end.
2. **"RTH close" (last bar before the regular close).** The close of the last 1-minute bar that starts before the regular close on the session's own calendar day. MES: regular close 15:00 CT, 12:15 CT on calendar early-close days; bars from 08:30 (exactly `bars._sessions`, the close that `build_days` uses for prev_close). ZN: its day-session close, 14:00 CT (`multi.SPECS["ZN"]`), and 12:15 CT on calendar early-close days (the data stop at 12:14 then); bars from 07:20 CT.
3. **Front contracts.** MES: the workbench volume roll (`volume_roll_date` in roll_volume_check.csv, the file `--roll volume` uses). ZN: `multi.volume_roll` run over every frozen-calendar session up to 2024-12-31, early-close days included (multi.load_days drops early-close days for trading; the roll itself depends only on volume).
4. **Chaining across a roll.** The roll day R is the first session on the new front (MES `volume_roll_date`; ZN the session from which `multi.volume_roll` switches). Month-to-date return from start session S (previous month's L) to end session E (L-3): walk the sessions S..E; each time the front changes at R, close the running segment on the old contract at R's close and start the new one at the new contract's close on R. Return = product over segments of close(segment end) / close(segment start) on that segment's contract, minus 1. Computed exactly (fractions of integer ticks). Any close needed by this formula that is missing makes the month's signal missing (no T1 trade that month).
5. **rel and the T1 side.** rel = MES return - ZN return (exact). rel > 0: short on L-2, L-1 and L; rel < 0: long; rel == 0 or missing: no trade ($0) on all three. May 2019 has no signal because its start session (30 April 2019) is before the calendar and the data. The signal is the MES one for both markets: the MNQ diagnostic trades MNQ on the same MES-minus-ZN rel ("same rules").
6. **Trades.** Entry at the open of the 08:30 CT bar, exit at the open of the 15:00 CT bar, or of the 12:00 CT bar on calendar early-close days (frozen calendar). No stop; minutes in between do not matter. A missing entry or exit bar on a trade day makes that day non-evaluable (strategy and control together). The traded contract is the volume-roll front of the traded market on that day (`build_days`, `roll_column="volume_roll_date"`). T2 trades L of December 2024 but not F1..F3 of January 2025 (exam period, never loaded).
7. **Every session is in the table.** Sessions that are not trade positions return `no_trade` ($0) even without bars, so the workbench table holds every practice session of the calendar (none is dropped as ineligible). Non-evaluable trade days are $0 in the floor's numerator and stay in its denominator; they are counted and reported.
8. **Gates (spec `gates`).** Trade days = evaluable sessions with a trade. (a) beats luck: `inference.paired_test` (9,999 replicates, seed 20261027, mean block 5, one-sided) on L3 net per trade day vs the mean of the 500 control draws (L3 net) on the same days, at the spec bar alpha = 0.0253 for each trial. The workbench honesty counter multiplies FAMILY_SPLIT (2) by the distinct trials logged, so the CLI prints a stricter bar once both trials and the MNQ diagnostics are logged; the spec bar governs and both are reported. (b) floor: total L3 net / number of practice sessions >= $1.00 AND mean L3 net per trade day >= $5.00. Sharpe (annualised, of daily L3 net over all sessions) is reported, not gated. The generic workbench verdict (all evaluable days, its own floor) is not the spec verdict.
9. **Control.** For each trade day: `Generator(PCG64(SeedSequence([20261029, ROOT_CODE[root], yyyymmdd, 13])))`, `integers(0, 2, size=500) * 2 - 1` (1 long, 0 short), the same entry and exit bars and costs. Both trials use tag 13 (the spec gives one tag), so on a day both trade the draws are identical. A non-trade day has 0 in every draw.
10. **Diagnostics (not gated).** (i) T2 vs always-long: long 1 MES on every practice session that is not L, F1, F2 or F3, same bars and exit rule, L3 net; mean per session and a Welch t of the difference in means vs T2's trade days (descriptive). (ii) Per calendar year: trade days, total net, $/trade and $/session (that year's sessions). (iii) MNQ, both trials, same rules, control root 1. (iv) T1 hit rate (gross > 0) by sign of rel: rel > 0 (short) and rel < 0 (long). Costs L1-L4 are reported per trade and per session.
11. **Run order.** MES T1, MES T2 (gated), then MNQ T1, MNQ T2 (diagnostics).
