# SC-FUT-0001 — proposed remaining defaults for Jason's freeze review

**Review list only. Nothing below is frozen or authorization to calculate ORB P&L.** User decisions already on card: $15 net/MNQ/eligible day including no trade $0; no lower-confidence-bound gate added; missing-stop-minute stress *bound*, not an observed fill; same-close double break impossible; missing ATR history excludes session without looking at outcomes; round exact tick midpoint half-up. Entries by **14:30 CT fill minute**, not merely signal time.

| # | Open item | Narrow proposal for approval | Why/remaining risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | True range | On the traded dated contract, use prior *CME trade-date* session high/low/last observed regular trading close; `max(H-L, abs(H-prior_close), abs(L-prior_close))` for each of 14 completed sessions, requiring prior close for each. Never stitch contracts. If missing close, or <14 valid prior sessions, candidate day ineligible from data only. | Explicit warm-up may exclude many early dates after rolls; audit those counts first, not by returns. Official vs last-trade close must be decided. |
| 2 | Time and range | Bar timestamps label minute start. 08:30–08:44 CT bars complete by 08:45; earliest signal bar starts 08:45, is known at 08:46; entry at the *next* bar open 08:46 if present. No new entry fill after 14:30; last candidate signal bar starts 14:29. | Assert monotonic bar availability and no 08:45 simultaneous execution. |
| 3 | Order priority | One position at a time; on completed exit, the opposite unused side may later signal. No re-entry on an already used side; close cannot be both `> high` and `< low` if high>=low—assert rather than model a double break. Entry-bar low/high touching stop is adverse stop-first. | Resolve whether an opposite signal on the exact exit bar is allowed (recommend next bar only). |
| 4 | Tick rounding | Distance from `0.1 × ATR` rounded to nearest 0.25 point, exact half tick **up** in positive distance; minimum one tick. Long stop entry minus rounded distance; short stop entry plus rounded distance. | Chosen before results, not optimized by outcome. |
| 5 | Missing minutes with live stop | Track every absent minute from expected schedule while position+stop are live. Stress bound at worse of stop and next *visible* bar's adverse low (long) or high (short). It is **not a fill** and is never put into the observed ledger as one. Record impacted eligible sessions/trades and conditional effect; if no subsequent visible bar before flatten mark non-evaluable. | Proposed alarm **>1% of eligible sessions OR >1% of executed trades** affected => affected result inconclusive. | 6 | Missing entry/flatten | If the very next entry minute is absent, skip that candidate, log reason, do not chase next visible open. | 8 | Control construction | Primary control: 500 seeded draws per eligible day from observed legal entry minutes, random direction; one prespecified per-day **mean of 500 control P&Ls** paired with ORB day. On ORB no-trade days control P&L $0 to match exposure. | Conditioning controls on realized ORB entry count is a design choice that must be approved; could mask signal-frequency value.
